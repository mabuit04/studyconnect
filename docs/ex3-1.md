#Ranking UX (User experience) Factors 
  from most important to least important


  
## 1 Pragmatic Quality
  The product is practical and functional overall.
  ### Reason: 
    If a product does not fulfil a specific function there is no reason to use the product.
  
## 2 Controllability
  The product always responds to user interaction in a predictable and consistent way.
  ### Reason: 
    A product that does not behave the way it should, is hard to use to achieve goals.
  
## 3 Appearance
  The product is attractive, beautiful and/or designed in an appealing way.  
  ### Reason: 
    There is no fun in looking at a product with no matching colors and styles. 
  
## 4 Trust
  The product appears trustworthy to the user.
  ### Reason:
    Today it is important to show the user that his data is securely handled (even if it's not).
  
## 5 Ease of Use
  The product is easy to operate.
  ### Reason:
    The easier it is to use the more people can and will use it.
  
## 6 Efficiency
  The user can reach their goals with minimum time required and
  minimum physical effort.
  ### Reason:
    People want to save time by using a product. A product that wastes the time of the user is not successful.

## 7 Helpfulness
  The product helps the user.
  ### Reason:
    A good product helps the user with recommendations on using the product the correct/efficient way.
  
## 8 Completeness
  The user considers the information and/or functionality
  provided and/or offered to the user by the product to be complete.
  ### Reason:
    No one expects a product to work completely in the first version (e.g. Beta Versions of Computer Games).
