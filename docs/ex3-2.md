#  UX Factors

| Nr |  Reaction | UX Factor |
|:-----------:|------------------|---------------|
| 1 | **That is exactly what I needed.** | Completeness |
| 2 | **It makes life easier.** | Helpfulness |
| 3 | **I´m in control.” or “I am the boss.** | Controllability/Dependability |
| 4 | **We know each other.** | Identity |
| 5 | **Not being alone.** | Social Influences |
| 6 | **Wow.** | Originality |
| 7 | **Something went wrong.** | Pragmatic Quality |
| 8 | **I´ve never seen anything like this before.** | Novelity |
